## Recipes

### Monday: Spicy Peanut Noodle Bowls with Crispy Tofu

**Serves:** 3  
**Active:** 25 min  **Total:** 40 min

**Ingredients:**

- firm tofu: 14 oz
- rice noodles: 8 oz
- natural peanut butter: 3 tbsp
- sriracha: 1 tsp
- lime juice: 2 tbsp
- tamari: 2 tbsp
- maple syrup: 1 tsp
- garlic: 2 cloves
- carrots: 1 cup
- cucumber: 1 whole
- edamame: 1/2 cup
- cilantro: 1/4 cup

**Steps:**

1. Press tofu for 10 min, then cube and pan-fry until golden.
2. Cook rice noodles, drain, toss with peanut-sriracha-lime dressing.
3. Shred carrots, slice cucumber, chop cilantro.
4. Assemble bowls with noodles, tofu, veggies, and dressing.

> **Variations:**
> - **Isla:** Serve deconstructed: plain noodles, tofu, veggies, peanut sauce.

### Wednesday: Coconut Chickpea Curry
