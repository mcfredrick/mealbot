## Recipes

### Monday: Red Lentil & Coconut Dal with Cauliflower

**Serves:** 3  
**Active:** 15 min  **Total:** 35 min

**Ingredients:**

- red lentils: 1.5 cups
- coconut milk: 1 can
- cauliflower florets: 1 head
- turmeric: 1 tsp
- ginger: 1 tbsp
- chili flakes: 1 tsp

**Steps:**

1. Rinse lentils and simmer with coconut milk, turmeric, and ginger until soft.
2. Steam cauliflower florets until tender.
3. Serve dal over cauliflower or rice.
4. Add chili flakes to individual bowls for spice.

> **Variations:**
> - **Matt:** Add extra chili flakes for spice.
> - **Lara:** Add chili flakes for spice.
> - **Isla:** Ensure cauliflower is very soft and omit chili flakes.

### Tuesday: Sheet Pan Salmon and Rainbow Veggies

**Serves:** 3  
**Active:** 15 min  **Total:** 30 min

**Ingredients:**

- salmon fillets: 2 fillets
- extra firm tofu: 1 block
- bell peppers: 2 whole
- broccoli florets: 2 cups
- olive oil: 2 tbsp
- lemon juice: 1 whole

**Steps:**

1. Preheat oven to 400F.
2. Place salmon and tofu cubes on one side of a large sheet pan, and veggies on the other.
3. Drizzle all with olive oil and lemon juice (skip salt).
4. Roast for 20 minutes until salmon is cooked and tofu is firm.

> **Variations:**
> - **Matt:** Use tofu instead of salmon.
> - **Lara:** Use salmon.
> - **Isla:** Cut veggies and tofu into bite-sized pieces; ensure salmon is flaky.

### Wednesday: GF Corn Pasta with Creamy Avocado Sauce

**Serves:** 3  
**Active:** 15 min  **Total:** 25 min

**Ingredients:**

- gluten-free corn pasta: 12 oz
- ripe avocados: 2 whole
- fresh basil: 0.5 cup
- cherry tomatoes: 1 cup
- nutritional yeast: 2 tbsp

**Steps:**

1. Boil gluten-free pasta according to package instructions.
2. Blend avocado, basil, and nutritional yeast with a splash of pasta water to make a sauce.
3. Toss pasta with sauce and halved cherry tomatoes.
4. Serve warm or cold.

> **Variations:**
> - **Matt:** Add nutritional yeast for cheesy flavor without dairy.
> - **Lara:** Top with grilled shrimp if desired (extra ingredient).
> - **Isla:** Ensure tomatoes are quartered very small to avoid choking hazard.

### Thursday: Black Bean and Sweet Potato Grain Bowls

**Serves:** 3  
**Active:** 20 min  **Total:** 40 min

**Ingredients:**

- sweet potatoes: 2 large
- black beans: 1 can
- quinoa: 1 cup
- avocado: 1 whole
- lime: 1 whole
- salsa: 0.5 cup

**Steps:**

1. Roast cubed sweet potatoes until tender.
2. Cook quinoa according to package instructions.
3. Rinse black beans.
4. Assemble bowls with quinoa, beans, sweet potato, and avocado slices.
5. Top with lime juice and salsa (check salsa for salt/sugar).

> **Variations:**
> - **Matt:** Add hot sauce for spice.
> - **Lara:** Add optional shredded chicken.
> - **Isla:** Mash avocado and sweet potato for easier eating.

### Friday: Thai-Style Red Curry with Tofu

**Serves:** 3  
**Active:** 25 min  **Total:** 40 min

**Ingredients:**

- extra firm tofu: 1 block
- coconut milk: 1 can
- red curry paste: 2 tbsp
- snap peas: 1 cup
- bell peppers: 1 whole
- jasmine rice: 1 cup

**Steps:**

1. Cook jasmine rice.
2. Sauté curry paste in a pan with a little coconut milk.
3. Add remaining coconut milk, sliced peppers, and snap peas.
4. Add cubed tofu and simmer until vegetables are tender.
5. Serve over rice.

> **Variations:**
> - **Matt:** Increase curry paste for extra heat.
> - **Lara:** Add shrimp to portion.
> - **Isla:** Cut snap peas into very thin slivers; ensure tofu is soft.

### Saturday: Mediterranean Stuffed Bell Peppers

**Serves:** 3  
**Active:** 20 min  **Total:** 45 min

**Ingredients:**

- large bell peppers: 3 whole
- cooked quinoa: 2 cups
- chickpeas: 1 can
- dried oregano: 1 tsp
- zucchini: 1 medium

**Steps:**

1. Halve bell peppers and remove seeds.
2. Mix cooked quinoa, chickpeas, finely diced zucchini, and oregano.
3. Stuff peppers with the mixture.
4. Bake at 375F for 25-30 minutes until peppers are soft.

> **Variations:**
> - **Matt:** Add extra oregano and nutritional yeast to filling.
> - **Lara:** Add ground lamb to the filling mix.
> - **Isla:** Ensure zucchini is finely minced; serve pepper flesh deconstructed if preferred.

### Sunday: Sweet Potato & Black Bean Quesadillas

**Serves:** 3  
**Active:** 20 min  **Total:** 30 min

**Ingredients:**

- corn tortillas: 6 whole
- sweet potatoes: 1 large
- black beans: 0.5 can
- dairy-free cheese: 1 cup
- spinach: 1 handful

**Steps:**

1. Mash cooked sweet potato.
2. Spread mash on corn tortillas, add black beans, spinach, and dairy-free cheese.
3. Fold and pan-fry until crispy.
4. Serve with avocado slices.

> **Variations:**
> - **Matt:** Ensure dairy-free cheese is used.
> - **Lara:** Add shredded chicken inside.
> - **Isla:** Cut into small, easy-to-hold triangles; ensure no large bean chunks.
